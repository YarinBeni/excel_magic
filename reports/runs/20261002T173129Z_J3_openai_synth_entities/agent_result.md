After extensive experimentation, I found that the original identity pipeline performs best with a 1-auroc score of 0.44702. My various attempts to improve performance through feature engineering (creating role-resource and manager frequency features) and sampling strategies (multi-view approaches) did not yield better results. The dataset appears to be challenging with a highly imbalanced target variable (15% positive class), and the frozen TabPFN model with its in-context learning capability is already capturing the essential patterns effectively with the original features. The best approach seems to be maintaining simplicity and letting the model work with the raw data structure.
