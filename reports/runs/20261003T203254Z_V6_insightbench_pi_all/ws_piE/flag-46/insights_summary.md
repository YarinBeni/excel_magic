# Travel Expense Analysis Insights

## Key Findings

1. **High Rejection Rate**: Travel expenses have a 41.5% rejection rate (61 out of 147 travel claims declined), indicating significant compliance or processing issues.

2. **Duplicate Claims**: There are 3 expense numbers that appear twice in the dataset, suggesting potential duplicate submissions that could lead to overpayment.

3. **Departmental Disparities**: IT department has the highest decline rate at 54.55% followed by HR at 50.00%, suggesting department-specific issues with travel expense processing.

4. **Outlier Expenses**: Several travel expenses exceed the 95th percentile amount ($7,630+), with the highest being $7,984, which may be contributing to rejections due to unusual spending.

5. **Monthly Trends**: Decline rates vary by month, with peaks in November (57.14%), February (47.06%), and March (53.85%), suggesting seasonal or temporal factors affecting approvals.

6. **High-Value Rejected Claims**: The top 10 declined travel expenses range from $5,985 to $7,798, with several exceeding $7,000, indicating potential issues with large expense submissions.

7. **Duplicate Patterns**: Two specific combinations of amount and description appear twice (amount $6,234 with "Travel hardware equipment" and amount $1,322 with "Automatically generated expense line for creation of travel"), suggesting possible duplicate entries.

8. **Processing State Distribution**: Among travel expenses, 41.5% are submitted, 36.3% are pending, 36.3% are processed, and 41.5% are declined, showing significant variance in processing outcomes.
