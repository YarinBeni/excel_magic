# Goal Achievement Analysis Insights

## Key Findings

1. **Department Performance Disparity**: The IT department demonstrates the highest goal completion rate at 65.1%, significantly outperforming other departments (Marketing: 44.6%, HR: 44.1%, Finance: 40.6%).

2. **Priority Impact on Success**: Critical priority goals have the highest completion rate at 72.3%, followed by High priority at 65.9%. Medium and Low priority goals show much lower completion rates at 38.3% and 37.2% respectively.

3. **Target Percentage Effect**: Goals with low target percentages (0-59%) have the highest completion rate at 70.2%, suggesting that more achievable goals are more likely to be completed successfully.

4. **Metric Performance**: Survey Score and Expense Ratio metrics show the highest completion rates at 50.8% and 50.0% respectively, indicating these types of goals are more easily achieved.

5. **IT Department Excellence**: The IT department achieves a completion ratio of 107.6% when comparing average percent complete to target percentage, meaning they exceed their targets on average, showing exceptional performance.

6. **Priority vs Completion Rate**: Despite having the highest completion rate, Critical priority goals make up only 17% of all goals, while Low priority goals account for 31% of all goals, suggesting a potential misalignment between priority assignment and actual outcomes.

7. **Goal State Distribution**: Completed goals make up 48.4% of all goals, with In Progress (36.0%) and Planned (11.3%) representing the majority of active goals.

8. **Performance by Metric**: Employee Turnover Rate metric shows the highest completion rate among all metrics (46.4%), while Sales Increase shows the lowest (46.6%).
