# Analysis of TTR (Time To Resolution) Trends

## Key Findings

### 1. Overall TTR Trend
The average Time To Resolution (TTR) has dramatically increased from 3.2 days in January 2023 to over 35 days by December 2023, showing a concerning upward trend in incident resolution times.

### 2. Category Impact
Hardware incidents show the longest average TTR at 26.2 days, followed by Database (25.8 days), Inquiry / Help (25.3 days), Software (25.3 days), and Network (23.9 days).

### 3. Assignment Group Impact
Hardware and Software assignment groups show the highest average TTR at 27.3 and 27.2 days respectively, compared to Network (23.9 days) and other groups.

### 4. Priority Impact
While 2-High priority incidents have the highest average TTR (25.1 days), there's minimal difference between priority levels, suggesting that priority classification isn't strongly correlated with resolution speed.

### 5. Seasonal Pattern
There's a consistent monthly increase in TTR throughout 2023, with the most significant growth occurring in the latter half of the year, particularly in November and December.

### 6. Outliers
Several incidents show extremely long resolution times (37 days), with most being related to Hardware, Database, and Network categories, suggesting systemic issues in these areas.

## Recommendations

1. Focus on improving resolution processes for Hardware and Database categories
2. Investigate the root cause of the consistent monthly increase in TTR
3. Examine why incidents in the latter half of 2023 take significantly longer to resolve
4. Review resource allocation and skill sets for Hardware and Software teams
5. Implement proactive monitoring for long-running incidents to prevent escalation
