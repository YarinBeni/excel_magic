# Insights on Cost Reduction Goals in Finance Department

## Key Findings:

1. **Extended Durations**: The average duration of Cost Reduction goals in the Finance department is 259 days, with some goals lasting up to 382 days, showing significant delays.

2. **Increasing Trend Over Time**: There's a clear upward trend in goal durations throughout 2022, with average durations increasing from 171 days in January to 371 days in December.

3. **Incomplete Goals**: Among the 108 Finance Cost Reduction goals, 60 are currently "In Progress" with an average completion rate of only 42%, suggesting substantial delays or scope creep.

4. **Priority Doesn't Affect Duration**: All priority levels (Low, Medium, High, Critical) show similar average durations (~250-270 days), indicating that priority level isn't a strong predictor of duration.

5. **State Distribution**: 12 goals were cancelled, 60 are in progress, 27 completed, and 9 planned, showing a high proportion of unfinished goals.

6. **Duration by State**: Cancelled goals had the longest average duration (276 days), followed by In Progress (261 days) and Completed (256 days).

These insights suggest systemic issues in goal management within the Finance department, particularly with long-term planning and execution of cost reduction initiatives.
