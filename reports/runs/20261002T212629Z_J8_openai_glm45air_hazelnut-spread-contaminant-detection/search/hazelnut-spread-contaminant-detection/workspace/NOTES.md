Best score: 0.00720 (1-auroc) achieved with Yeo-Johnson power transformation in preprocessing. The transformation handled feature skewness and standardized the distribution, which was crucial for this microwave sensing contamination detection task. Other approaches including feature engineering, multiple context views, robust scaling, and postprocessing calibration either failed to improve or worsened the performance. The key insight was that proper data preprocessing alone was sufficient to achieve optimal performance without complex feature engineering.
